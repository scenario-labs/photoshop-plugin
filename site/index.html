    Scenario for Photoshop

    Scenario, inside Photoshop.

    A Photoshop panel for Scenario's image, edit, upscale, layer and video models. It reads the selection, layer or takes you are working on, shows the price on the button, and lands every result as layers you can mask, transform and undo in one step.

    Install the development build Follow one picture Read the guide Source on GitHub
    Development build 0.7.1. Not on the Adobe Marketplace yet: you build the .ccx from the repository. Photoshop 2026, tested on 27.11 on macOS. Open source, MIT.

    RED HORIZON

    One picture, eight steps.

    RED HORIZON is a poster for a science-fiction feature: an explorer on Mars holding her helmet in both hands, her six-wheeled expedition rover, a red plain under rock arches. Each step pairs a Photoshop tool with a Scenario action. Photoshop does what it already does well, Scenario adds what those tools cannot do alone, and the result is Photoshop material the next tool works on at once.

    The rover in a new livery

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopExpand, Feather+ScenarioSelect by description, Replace inside with BolderSAM 3.1 Image, GPT Image 2.5 Flare
    1. Type what to select in the context card's Select… field, "the rover", and click Select. The masks the model finds become one Photoshop selection. Selecting is a run of its own: 6 CU here.
    2. Photoshop: Select › Modify › Expand, then Feather, so the new livery has room and a soft edge. The card follows the new size.
    3. Choose Replace inside, set Edit to Bolder and describe the change: "The same six-wheeled expedition rover, the same shape, size, wheels, dish antenna…". Replace inside · 23 CU for two takes.
    4. The kept take lands on its own layer with a mask in the selection's shape. Try Inside selection runs the other mode on the same selection.

    Bolder may let the new subject reach past the selection, and the mask then grows over what the model drew. Here it also reshaped parts of the rover's body: place, size, wheels and dish held.

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopLasso, Feather+ScenarioReplace inside with BolderGPT Image 2.5 Flare

    3. The outpost lands inside the lasso's shape, on its own masked layer.

    Turn her around with Make versions

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopType, Free Transform+ScenarioMake versions, Remove backgroundGPT Image 2.5 Flare, 851 Labs Background Remover
    1. The sheet is a Photoshop document: cream paper, the title, and one type layer per label.
    2. Make versions on GPT Image 2.5 Flare, with a prompt per view, draws her from the front, in a 90° side profile and from the back: "The same woman alone, in the same worn white and burnt-orange expedition spacesuit…". Make versions · 23 CU for two takes.
    3. Keep a take, then Remove background on it, from the kept take's More. It lands as a mask, so the hidden pixels stay and you can paint the mask verified live.
    4. Place each view on the sheet's ground line with Edit › Free Transform. No run touches the type layers.

    A prop inside a marquee

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopRectangular Marquee, 400 × 520+ScenarioNew layer, Remove backgroundGPT Image 2.5 Flare, 851 Labs Background Remover
    →

    Draw a marquee where the prop goes, choose New layer and describe it: "her round expedition helmet on its own…". The 400 × 520 marquee is about the size of her torso on the sheet. The take is sized to the selection and placed inside it, so the label and the paper stay visible around it verified live. Here the kept take still carried a background, cleared with Remove background on the take, 1 CU.

    Lands: Cut out, the default, asks the model for a transparent background. When the model still returns a full picture, Photoshop's own Remove Background cuts it at placement, as a layer mask you can paint. Full frame keeps the model's background inside the selection box. Without a selection, New layer makes a new image on a layer above the active one.

    New angle

    With a layer active, More › Make variants › New angle turns the subject to a preset angle with no prompt: Turn left 45° Turn right 45° Side view Back view or From above, on Qwen Edit Multiangle 2511. A kept take's More offers it too, on the take that is showing verified live. Neither film uses it: their side and back views come from Make versions, with a prompt per view.

    What came back: a clean background and three elements, shown here on a checkerboard; in Photoshop each sits where it was in the frame. The prompt listed "the explorer and her long shadow, the rover and its shadow, the research outpost". Split into layers · 28 CU.

    • On a flat image, Split into layers is the first chip; on a layer it is in More › Layers. Leave the prompt empty for the main elements, or list them.

    • The split leaves the long cast shadows behind: the elements come back without them, and the clean background has none. It did so on all four runs.

    Every orange stripe, one adjustment

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopHue/Saturation adjustment layer+ScenarioSelect by descriptionSAM 3.1 Image

    The suit and the rover change together: one selection, one adjustment layer, one slider.

    1. Type "orange stripe" in the Select… field: SAM 3.1 Image finds 20 objects across her suit and the rover, and they become one Photoshop selection.
    2. Photoshop: Layer › New Adjustment Layer › Hue/Saturation. The selection becomes the adjustment's mask.
    3. Move Hue. The four liveries are the picture as made and that one layer at −170, +150 and +25.

    One adjustment per layer

    THE LAST KEEPERPhotoshopHue/Saturation, clipped+ScenarioSplit into layers, Select by descriptionSeedream 5.0 Flash Layerize, SAM 3.1 Image

    On a split picture, Photoshop does the grade one layer at a time: Layer › New Adjustment Layer › Hue/Saturation with Use Previous Layer to Create Clipping Mask. On THE LAST KEEPER the lighthouse goes blue and the rowing boat yellow; her raincoat, found with Select by description, goes red. Each adjustment touches one element, so the rest of the painting stays as it was. Its split needed one fix by hand: the lighthouse came back 58 px from its place, and was moved back with Move and its edge tidied with a layer mask.

    Relight to scene (More › Make variants) relights a cut-out or a split element to match what is under it, masked to its outline, with GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst. Neither film uses it. It can redraw details: name what must stay in the prompt and look at every take at 100%.

    Split text

    For a title painted into the pixels, a flattened file or a client's JPEG: More › Layers › Split text returns the clean base and the text on its own layer above it, Ideogram V3 Layerize Text, no prompt. The base is ready for a new title set with the Type tool. Remove text (More › Finish) gives only the clean base. Neither film's poster needs it: their titles are live type from the start.

    PhotoshopThe Type tool, Move

    Six lines of live type over the art; then Move takes the billing clear of her boots.

    +
    ScenarioFormats

    Smart Reframe to 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, with a prompt for what must stay in frame.

    =
    ResultFour artboards, one document

    Each with its six lines laid out again, still editable type.

    RED HORIZONPhotoshopType tool, Move+ScenarioFormatsSmart Reframe

    The four artboards of one document, each fitted to its cell. On every artboard the six lines are live type. On 9:16, the right edge of the frame cuts through the outpost.

    1. Set the poster's type with the Type tool: a studio line, the title, a tagline, two billing lines and a release line, each its own type layer.
    2. Select the art and choose Formats, a chip on a flat image, More › Finish on a layer. Pick the format in the Format chip.
    3. Optional: say what must stay in frame, "Keep the explorer, the rover and the research outpost whole in frame, at their size". Formats · 45 CU per format.
    4. The picture is reframed without its type layers. Each line is laid out again on the artboard at its place in the frame, scaled with the picture, smaller only when it would not fit. Run the next format: it is added to the same document.
    5. Then Photoshop: on the 1:1 and 16:9 artboards, the Move tool takes the billing lines clear of her boots.

    Four artboards in one document, each title live inside its artboard verified live on 0.7.1. Text painted into the pixels is reframed with the picture and can still be cut: use Split text first.

    Extend canvas

    To grow the poster itself to a wider ratio, keeping its pixels and drawing only the new area, use Extend canvas: a chip on a layer, More › Finish on a flat image. Pick the target in Extend to. The default model, Reframe, redraws the frame around the original; for +20% each side, pick GPT Image 2.5 Flare. Neither film uses it: their wide versions come from Formats.

    Look at the old edges at 100%. Runs in testing showed a step at the old edge or drew a figure twice; the checks flag a step or a repeated detail but do not remove it. Extend in smaller steps if you see one.

    SwitchOff (default)On
    Agents may modify the documentEvery result an agent wants to land shows a proposal card with Apply and DismissResults land at once as one History step "Scenario agent: <action>", with an Undo banner

    A typical edit, and where you approve

    1. document_summaryreads
    2. set_selectionfirst switch
    3. capture_reference
    4. upload_referenceUpload card: before an agent first sends pixels from a document in a session tested automated
    5. estimate_costfree
    6. generateSpend card: the client, the model, the prompt, the price on Approve verified live

    • Spend card. Decline spends nothing. The agent must send back the exact price it was quoted; a changed price or an expired quote is refused before anything is spent. A card left unanswered expires after 10 minutes.
    • Proposal card. Shows the client, the model, the target document and the History name; it shows no picture of the result in this build.
    • Agent spending. Ask each time, the default, or Allow for one client session, with a limit per call and a total. End stops it at once. The Activity log lists every agent request.
    • Agents change only the document in front; the plugin never brings another document forward for them. Their jobs show in Jobs, marked with the client's name.

    Install

    Build it, install it, sign in.

    Photoshop 2026 (27.x)macOS tested, Windows not testedNode.js 20 or later to buildScenario plan with API access, Starter or aboveProject key, Editor role

    The plugin is not on the Adobe Marketplace yet. You build the .ccx package from the repository and install it with Creative Cloud's installer.

    1. Build the package from a checkout of scenario-labs/photoshop-plugin: 
      npm ci
npm run package   # writes build/scenario-photoshop-0.7.1.ccx
    2. Install it with Creative Cloud's plugin installer, then restart Photoshop: 
      "/Library/Application Support/Adobe/Adobe Desktop Common/RemoteComponents/UPI/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent.app/Contents/MacOS/UnifiedPluginInstallerAgent" \
  --install "$PWD/build/scenario-photoshop-0.7.1.ccx"
    3. Open the panel from Plugins › Scenario. Photoshop hides docked panels on its Home screen, so make or open a document first: File › New is enough.

    The development package installs this way verified live. Installing the release package, by command or by double-click, and the time to a first image not tested.

    Sign in

    1. On app.scenario.com, in the project you want to use, create a Project key with the Editor role. Copy the key and its secret. Only team admins and project owners can create keys: Copy a request for your admin prepares the message.
    2. Paste both in the panel and click Save and test. Pasting key:secret fills both fields. Two free reads check the key.
    Where the key is keptPhotoshop's secure storage for this plugin, one entry per project, under your operating system account. It goes only to Scenario's API over HTTPS, never to the helper or to agents.
    Another projectAdd a key for another project, then click the project name in the panel header to switch.
    A spending capA limit on the key, set on app.scenario.com, caps what the plugin and connected agents can spend.
    Sign outRemoves the key from this computer only. Delete the key on app.scenario.com to revoke it.

    Signing in verified live through the development build's key import. Typing or pasting into the fields, and the screens for a refused key or a plan without API access not tested live.

    2. Context card. What the panel works on and where the result lands. During a run it shows the progress.
    3. Select… Select by description.
    4. Action chips. At most six for this context, then More with the others in groups: Finish, Select and cut, Layers, Make variants, Animate. A pin in More adds an action to the chips.
    5. + Reference. The selection's box, a layer, all visible, a file or a Library image.
    6. Prompt. An action that needs none says so.
    7. Suggest. Writes a prompt that fits the action and the size the result fills.
    8. Chips. Style, model and takes, each showing its value. The gear holds the quality level, Medium by default, and advanced options.
    9. The action button with its price, Create image · 47 CU for four takes at quality High.

    Prices

    The price is on the button.

    Each change of prompt, model or setting is priced for free, a dry run that creates no job, about half a second later. The button shows it exact, as a range, or as "from" a price. Prices are in Scenario credits and vary by model, document and settings; the ones below are buttons from RED HORIZON's runs.

    Create image · 47 CU4 takes, quality High
    Replace inside · 23 CUBolder, 2 takes
    Upscale ×2 · 10 CUno prompt
    Fill in the prompt to see the priceneeds a prompt
    • A click on a priced button is the approval. Above 100 CU, for the top of a range and for any "from" price, a card asks first.
    • The request is built again at the click. If the document, the selection or a setting changed since the price check, nothing is sent.
    • If the price changes once references or captures are uploaded, the panel stops and asks again. Nothing was spent verified live.
    • A failed job shows "Refunded by Scenario"; a model without refunds says so before you click. The plugin shows no balance or plan: check those on app.scenario.com.

    Jobs

    Every run is a job, kept on disk.

    Every run, yours or an agent's, is a job on this computer. Each job keeps what was asked, the price quoted and the price charged, and where its result went. Jobs is also where a result that did not land is placed by hand.

    WhereJobs in the panel header opens the newest jobs, each with its time and price, then All jobs: each job with its picture, status, the price quoted and the price charged.
    Written firstEach job is written to disk before anything is sent, so Jobs survives a restart verified live on 0.5.0.
    After a restartUploading or waiting jobs become not sent, nothing charged; generating jobs resume; finished ones download again; nothing lands by itself.
    UnknownA request that may or may not have reached Scenario. The plugin looks it up: Link continues a match; Mark not sent appears only when a full look-up found none. Nothing is paid twice tested automated.
    PlacePlace, Place in current document and Open as document land a finished result where you choose.

    Library

    Your project's images, one click away.

    • Place from Library, in More › Layers or on a blank canvas: a grid of the project's images with search and the filters All, Generated, Edited, Uploaded, then Show more.
    • Click an image to place it as a new layer at its own size, never enlarged, centered, in one History state verified live. + Reference › Library uses the same grid.
    • Everything the panel makes is also in your Scenario project, so the takes you did not keep are here too.
    • Search matches asset names, which are often not the words of the prompt. Dragging from the grid is not in this build.

    Privacy

    What leaves your computer, and when.

    To Scenario's API

    Your key and secret with every request, over HTTPS. Prompt, model and settings for price checks and runs. Pixels only at your click: the selection's box plus a margin, a layer or the document, the mask and your references, each upload under 4,000,000 bytes.

    Downloaded

    Results from Scenario's CDN, the only download host this build accepts, through signed links kept in memory, without your key.

    To an agent

    What a connected agent reads, including previews of your documents, goes to that agent's model provider, under your agreement with that provider. The helper never receives your key.

    ResultsSaved before they are placed, kept or not, in the plugin's output folder, and in your Scenario project.
    ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/UXP/PluginsStorage/PHSP/27/External/
  com.scenario.photoshop/PluginData/Scenario/<kind>/<date>/
    Layer metadataEach placed layer carries model, prompt, settings, job and prices in its XMP, which survives saving the PSD. Never your key, secret, token, team or project ids, or file paths.
    Key and tokenPhotoshop's secure storage. The helper's token file is readable by your account only.
    DiagnosticsVersions, switch states, job counts and recent error types, with no prompts, names, ids, paths, keys or tokens. Copy diagnostics shows the text before copying it.
    Local linkPort 9890 of the loopback interface only, which other computers cannot reach. Web pages are refused. The plugin and each helper prove they hold the pairing token without sending it.
    DeletingOn this computer: sign out, reset the pairing token, delete the plugin data folder. On Scenario: delete uploads and results in the web app, or write to support@scenario.com. Signing out does not revoke a key: delete it on app.scenario.com.

    The plugin sends nothing else: no update check, no analytics. Details: PRIVACY.md. Security reports: privately to support@scenario.com with "security" in the subject.

    Troubleshooting

    When something does not land.

    What you seeWhat to do
    The panel is empty on Photoshop's Home screenMake or open a document. Photoshop hides docked panels on Home.
    "Scenario did not accept this key and secret."Copy both again, from a Project key. Enter key again or Create an API key.
    "API keys need a Starter plan or above."Use a team on a plan with API access, or Open plans.
    A Team (Organization) key or a Reader keyUse a Project key with the Editor role.
    A card says the price changedNothing was spent. Approve at the new price or Cancel.
    A job is UnknownOpen Jobs and let it look the job up, then Link or Mark not sent.
    A result did not landBring the document it started from to the front, then Place in Jobs, or Open as document.
    "No connection to Scenario."Check the network, then Retry.
    "Photoshop is busy. Try again in a moment."Photoshop was placing or reading; the panel tried three times. Click again.
    A Request For Permission card from PhotoshopThe first link the panel opens to a new site asks Photoshop's permission; answer it.
    A subject cut at the edge of a formatName it in the Formats prompt and run that format again.

    Agents

    NotConnectedOpen the Scenario panel; the helper reaches it only while it is open.
    PairingFailedThe token changed. Run the setup command again from Connect a client.
    VersionMismatchThe plugin and helper versions differ. Connect a client shows the command for the new version.
    DocumentNotActiveBring the document forward, then ask the agent to retry.
    NoSpendTurn off No-spend mode in Settings.
    QuoteExpired, CostMismatchNothing was spent. The agent prices the run again.
    SpendDeclined, UploadDeclinedYou declined the card. Nothing was spent or sent.

    Still stuck: support@scenario.com, with the text from Copy diagnostics.

    Known limits

    What this build does not do yet, and what was not tested.

    Not in this build

    • Start a set, Same action on each, and Run a workflow.
    • Drag and drop onto the panel or from the Library grid.
    • The update check and an Output Folder setting.
    • The Adobe Marketplace listing and the helper on npm.
    • A picture of the result on the agent's proposal card.

    Look closely at

    • Extend canvas: a step or a repeated detail at the old edge.
    • Inside selection: a lighter or darker patch at the selection edge. Edges ok is a hint.
    • Split into layers: a small element can land in the wrong place without a note.
    • Split into layers: cast shadows can stay behind. Bring them back from the original with a layer mask.
    • New layer cut out: with several elements, Photoshop's Remove Background can keep the wrong one.
    • Relight to scene can redraw details: name what must stay.
    • Formats: text painted into the pixels is reframed with the picture and can be cut.

    Not tested

    • Windows, and Photoshop versions other than 27.11.
    • Installing the release .ccx, and the time from install to a first image.
    • Typing into the sign-in fields; the refused-key and no-API-access screens live.
    • Color-managed documents other than sRGB, 16-bit, CMYK, Lab, documents over about 8000 px, Undo and Redo of a landed result.
    • Match an image live; the trained Z-Image route and a stacked style.
    • Cursor and Claude Desktop sessions; a Codex session against Photoshop; two agent clients at once.
    • Panel widths other than 320 px on 0.7.x.
    • The panel following a Free Transform or a Move before New angle (tested automated only).
